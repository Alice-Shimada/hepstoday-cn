By traveling through the Earth's atmosphere and magnetic field, these particles create a noticeable effect on the electric field at the surface. In this article, we demonstrate that parameters of the shower longitudinal development can be inferred from mapping the time dependence of the observed electric field to the emitted electric field as a function of slant depth along the shower axis.
