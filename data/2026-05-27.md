### [1] [Search for a new heavy scalar resonance decaying to a pair of Z bosons in the four-lepton final state in proton-proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26462)
*CMS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: CMS实验在13 TeV质子-质子对撞中，利用138 fb⁻¹数据，搜索质量范围130 GeV–3 TeV、衰变至ZZ→4ℓ的新型重标量共振态，考虑窄宽/宽宽情形及ggF/VBF产生机制；未发现显著超出SM预期的信号，给出95% CL下σ×ℬ(ZZ)的上限：低质量区约0.05–0.1 pb，高质量区趋近于零。

*Seung-il Nam*
