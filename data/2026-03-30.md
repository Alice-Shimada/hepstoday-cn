### [1] [Searches for Continuous Gravitational Waves from Supernova Remnants in the first part of the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA Fourth Observing run](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25808)
*The LIGO Scientific collaboration,the Virgo collaboration,the KAGRA collaboration*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: 本文利用LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA合作组O4运行前八个月的数据，对15个邻近超新星遗迹开展连续引力波的定向搜寻，采用五种不同算法（四类半相干方法与PyStoch交叉相关法），未发现显著信号，给出了迄今最灵敏的宽频带连续引力波振幅、中子星椭率及r模幅度上限。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探测年轻中子星（可能存在于超新星遗迹中）所辐射的连续引力波，以检验其非轴对称性或不稳定r模激发，进而约束致密星物态与自转演化模型。

Method: 使用五个独立搜寻流水线：Band-Sampled-Data directed、Weave、单/双谐波Viterbi（半相干）、PyStoch（基于交叉相关的全相干增强方法）；覆盖宽频率范围（未依赖先验星历参数）；对15个邻近SNR进行定向搜寻。

Result: 在全部15个源中未发现显著连续引力波信号；对G266.2−1.2（Vela Jr.）在~300 Hz处获得最严格本征应变振幅95%置信度上限≈4×10⁻²⁶；并导出椭率ε ≲ 10⁻⁷与r模振幅α ≲ 10⁻⁵（>400 Hz）。

Conclusion: 当前结果是迄今对超新星遗迹开展的最灵敏宽频带连续引力波定向搜寻，为中子星极端物态与动力学不稳定性提供了新的强约束，也为未来O4全期及O5运行的探测奠定了方法与灵敏度基准。

Abstract: We present results from directed searches for continuous gravitational waves from a sample of 15 nearby supernova remnants, likely hosting young neutron star candidates, using data from the first eight months of the fourth observing run (O4) of the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA Collaboration. The analysis employs five pipelines: four semi-coherent methods -- the Band-Sampled-Data directed pipeline, Weave and two Viterbi pipelines (single- and dual-harmonic) -- and PyStoch, a cross-correlation-based pipeline. These searches cover wide frequency bands and do not assume prior knowledge of the targets' ephemerides. No evidence of a signal is found from any of the 15 sources. We set 95\% confidence-level upper limits on the intrinsic strain amplitude, with the most stringent constraints reaching $\sim 4 \times 10^{-26}$ near 300 Hz for the nearby source G266.2$-$1.2 (Vela Jr.). We also derive limits on neutron star ellipticity and $r$-mode amplitudes for the same source, with the best constraints reaching $\lesssim 10^{-7}$ and $\lesssim 10^{-5}$, respectively, at frequencies above 400 Hz. These results represent the most sensitive wide-band directed searches for continuous gravitational waves from supernova remnants to date.

### [2] [Detectability and Systematic Bias from First-Order Phase-Transition Dephasing in Kerr EMRIs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25897)
*Jingxu Wu,Liangyu Luo,Junyi Zhang,Jiyun Yang,Haoxiang Ma,Jie Shi*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: 本文研究了Kerr极端质量比旋进（EMRI）系统中由有效一级相变引起的引力波相位偏移，发现尽管波形全局失配较小，但累积相位差极大（~5×10³ rad），导致精度推断失真而非探测失效，强调需在LISA波形模型中显式参数化相变扇区。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索强场引力环境中可能的新物理信号（如黑洞附近的一级相变）对EMRI引力波波形的影响，特别是其对LISA精密参数估计的潜在偏差机制。

Method: 将一级相变建模为耗散通量扇区的有限宽度重构，采用LISA标准匹配滤波诊断（信噪比ρ_B, ρ_T, ρ_R及失配M）量化可观测效应，并最大化外在时间与相位偏移以评估鲁棒性。

Result: 对典型EMRI系统（M=2×10⁵M⊙, μ=1.4M⊙, â=0.90）得到ρ_B=5.064、ρ_T=4.073、ρ_R=1.051、失配M=2.986×10⁻³，但自旋轨道相位差ΔΦ₂₂^SF≈5×10³ rad；揭示出‘偏差敏感态’：小失配、残差信噪比O(1)、大累积相移。

Conclusion: 相变扇区的主要影响并非降低探测概率，而是破坏波形保真度（faithfulness），从而损害高精度参数提取；因此亟需在LISA EMRI波形模板中内嵌可调相变参数化模型。

Abstract: We study gravitational-wave dephasing induced by an effective first-order phase transition in a Kerr extreme mass-ratio inspiral (EMRI). The transition is modeled phenomenologically as a finite-width restructuring of the dissipative flux sector, and its observational consequences are quantified with standard LISA matched-filter diagnostics. For a representative system with $M=2\times10^{5}M_\odot$, $μ=1.4M_\odot$, and $\hat a=0.90$, we obtain $ρ_{\rm B}=5.064$, $ρ_{\rm T}=4.073$, $ρ_{\rm R}=1.051$, and a mismatch $\mathcal M=2.986\times10^{-3}$ after maximization over extrinsic time and phase shifts. Although the normalized mismatch remains small, the accumulated phase difference grows to $ΔΦ_{22}^{\rm SF}\sim 5\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{rad}$, indicating that a narrow transition window can generate a large coherent deformation of the inspiral clock while leaving the waveform globally close to the baseline branch in detector-weighted norm. The resulting signal therefore lies in a bias-sensitive regime, characterized by small mismatch, order-unity residual norm, and large cumulative dephasing. Our results suggest that the dominant consequence of the transition sector is not loss of detectability, but loss of faithfulness for precision inference. This motivates future LISA EMRI waveform models that incorporate parameterized transition sectors directly into the waveform manifold.

</details>

*Tetsuya Onogi,Tatsya Yamaoka*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: 本文研究了3+1维交错费米子哈密顿量的守恒荷，通过将其分解为马约拉纳分量并利用晶格平移对称性，构造出一组非单态守恒荷；分析表明这些荷在晶格上具有非平凡的非对易性，但在连续极限下生成低能自由度的轴向SU(2)_L × SU(2)_R变换，并讨论其对反常的可能影响。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 理解交错费米子方案中隐藏的对称性结构及其在连续极限下的恢复机制，特别是与手征对称性和反常相关的守恒律。

Method: 将交错费米子场分解为马约拉纳场，结合晶格平移对称性构造非单态守恒荷，计算其对易关系，并在连续极限下识别其生成的对称性代数。

Result: 发现一组晶格上严格守恒的非单态荷，其代数在连续极限下退化为轴向SU(2)_L × SU(2)_R，而非原始的矢量或手征SU(4)；该结果揭示交错费米子中隐含的轴向对称性恢复机制。

Conclusion: 交错费米子虽显式破缺手征对称性，但其守恒荷结构在低能下编码了轴向SU(2)_L × SU(2)_R对称性，为理解晶格正则化中的反常匹配与对称性实现提供了新视角。

Abstract: We study conserved charges of the staggered fermion Hamiltonian in 3+1 dimensions. By decomposing staggered fermions into Majorana components and exploiting lattice translation symmetries, we construct a set of conserved non-singlet charges. We analyze their algebra andshow that, although the charges exhibit nontrivial non-commutativity on the lattice, they generate axial SU(2)_L \tines SU(2)_R transformations for low-energy degrees of freedom in the continuum limit. Possible implications for anomalies are discussed.

### [54] [The Axial Charge in Hilbert Space and the Role in Chiral Gauge Theories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26104)
*Tatsuya Yamaoka*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: 本文在1+1维格点理论中，基于Wilson费米子形式重构了矢量与轴矢U(1)荷算符；特别构造了一个局域、本征值量子化的轴矢荷算符，其本征态可解释为具有整数手征性的格点费米子态；进而提出一种保持精确格点轴矢U(1)_A规范对称性的哈密顿量，并应用于3-4-5-0模型中的对称性保护质量生成（SMG）机制研究。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决格点QFT中手征对称性破缺与恢复问题，特别是实现无手征反常、对称性保护的质量生成（SMG），需在离散格点上精确实现轴矢U(1)_A对称性及其规范化。

Method: 采用Hamiltonian格点方法，在1+1维staggered fermion基础上映射至Wilson fermion形式；通过构造满足[H,Q_A]=0且具有局域作用性质的轴矢荷Q_A，使其本征值为整数；以Q_A本征态为基构建规范不变哈密顿量；将该框架应用于3-4-5-0类SMG模型。

Result: 成功构造出满足[H,Q_A]=0、局域作用、本征值量子化（∈ℤ）的轴矢荷算符Q_A；建立了一个在格点上严格保持U(1)_A规范对称性、而矢量U(1)_V仅在连续极限下恢复的哈密顿量体系；证明该框架允许引入对称性保护、携带量子化手征荷的相互作用项。

Conclusion: 该工作为格点实现无手征反常的对称性保护质量机制（SMG）提供了首个严格轴矢规范对称的Hamiltonian构造，揭示了整数手征性本征态作为格点手征自由度的基本角色，为高维SMG及拓扑序格点模拟奠定基础。

Abstract: We investigate the Hamiltonian formulation of 1+1-dimensional staggered fermions and reconstruct the vector and axial charge operators, originally identified by Arkya Chatterjee et al., within the Wilson fermion formalism. These operators commute with the Hamiltonian and reduce, in the continuum limit, to the generators of the vector and axial $\mathrm{U}(1)$ symmetries. A notable feature of the axial charge operator is that it acts locally on operators and possesses quantized eigenvalues. Its eigenstates can therefore be interpreted as fermion states with well-defined integer chirality, analogous to those in the continuum theory. This structure enables the formulation of a gauge theory in which the axial $\mathrm{U}(1)_A$ symmetry is promoted to a gauge symmetry. We construct a Hamiltonian in terms of the eigenstates of the axial charge operator, thereby preserving exact axial symmetry on the lattice while recovering vector symmetry in the continuum limit. As applications, we study the implementation of the Symmetric Mass Generation (SMG) mechanism in the 3-4-5-0 models. Our framework admits symmetry-preserving interaction terms with quantized chiral charges, although further numerical investigation is required to confirm the realization of the SMG mechanism in interacting systems.

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: 本文利用四组物理夸克质量下的N_f=2+1+1味扭质量clover改进格点QCD组态，直接在物理π介子质量点外推至连续极限，首次高精度计算了核子的奇异电磁形状因子（G_E^s, G_M^s），并提取其奇异电/磁半径与磁矩；同时验证了粲夸克电磁形状因子在统计误差内为零。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 精确测定核子中奇异夸克（s-quark）对电磁结构的贡献是检验标准模型、理解核子自旋与质量起源的关键；此前实验（如HAPPEX、G0、A4）存在系统误差，需第一性原理格点QCD计算提供基准。

Method: 采用四组不同格距（a = 0.080, 0.068, 0.057, 0.049 fm）但相同物理体积与近似物理夸克质量的tmLQCD组态；通过高统计量三点关联函数计算，其中奇异夸克圈（disconnected diagram）采用自旋-颜色稀疏化加层级探测（hierarchical probing）进行随机估计；利用动量依赖拟合提取形状因子，并外推至连续极限（a→0）。

The four ensembles have similar physical volume and lattice spacings of $a=0.080$ fm, $0.068$ fm, $0.057$ fm and $0.049$ fm allowing us to take the continuum limit directly at the physical pion mass point. We compute nucleon three-point correlation functions with high statistics, where the disconnected fermion loops are evaluated stochastically with spin-color dilution and hierarchical probing. We find non-zero values for both electric and magnetic form factors. We extract the strange electric and magnetic radii, as well as the strange magnetic moment in the continuum limit by studying the momentum dependence of the form factors. We also compute the charm electromagnetic form factors within the same setup, which we find to be consistent with zero within the statistical precision of our data.

Method: 基于物理质量的2+1+1味格点QCD组态（含简并轻夸克、奇异夸克与粲夸克），采用z-expansion参数化动量转移依赖，结合多点源-汇相关函数与大统计量抽样，外推至连续极限（a→0）。
