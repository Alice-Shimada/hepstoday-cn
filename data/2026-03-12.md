### [1] [Topological structure of the entanglement radius of Yang-Mills flux tubes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10255)

(iv) The Far-future facilities such as the Muon Collider and FCC require a further leap to $\sim$10\,ps, setting the agenda for the next decade of detector R\&D.
