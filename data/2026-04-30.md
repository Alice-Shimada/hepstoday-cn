### [1] [Search for new physics in $B \to K ππγ$ with Belle II data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26315)
*Sahil Saha*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文讨论了对 $B^0 \to K_S^0 \pi^+ \pi^- \gamma$ 衰变的时间依赖CP不对称性测量，强调需通过振幅分析区分由CP本征态（如 kaonic 共振）与非CP本征态主导的子过程，以提取新物理信号。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 时间依赖CP不对称性测量对超出标准模型（BSM）的新物理敏感，但需消除强相互作用导致的CP本征态与非CP本征态混叠带来的理论不确定性。

Method: 采用振幅分析（Amplitude Analysis）方法，对 $B \to K_{res} \gamma \to K \pi^+ \pi^- \gamma$ 过程建模，显式参数化 $K\pi\pi$ 不变质量谱中的共振结构（如 $K^*(892)$、$K_0^*(1430)$ 等）及其相对相位与强度，并约束其CP性质。

Result: 提出并论证了基于部分波分解（Partial Wave Analysis）与协方差矩阵驱动拟合的振幅分析框架，可分离CP本征态（如 $K^*(892)^0 \to K_S^0 \pi^0$ 类似分量）与非CP本征态（如 $K_0^*(1430)^0 \to K_S^0 \pi^0$ 的标量非本征贡献），从而提升CPV测量对新物理耦合的灵敏度。

Conclusion: 该振幅分析是将 $B^0 \to K_S^0 \pi^+ \pi^- \gamma$ 的CP不对称性转化为对新物理有效算符（如磁偶极跃迁 $C_7^{\prime}$ 或右手流 $C_8^{\prime}$）严格约束的必要前提。

Abstract: The measurement of the time-dependent CP-asymmetry of $B^0 \to K_S^0 π^+ π^- γ$ decays is sensitive to contributions from physics beyond the Standard Model. To translate this measurement into a constraint on new physics, it is necessary to distinguish between decay modes that contribute to the final state via a kaonic resonance that is a CP eigenstate, and those that proceed through non-CP eigenstates. This requires an amplitude analysis of the $B \to K_{res} γ\to K π^+ π^- γ$ decay, that is discussed in this article.

Furthermore, via the Hodge--GUE correspondence, we derive a fine structure in the $b = 2ν$ case, which implies a conjectural statement of Gharakhloo--Latimer.
