### [1] [Enhancing Angular Sensitivity of Segmented Antineutrino Detectors for Reactor Monitoring Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03561)
*Brian C. Crow,Max A. A. Dornfest,John G. Learned,Jackson D. Seligman,Nathan S. Sibert,Jeffrey G. Yepez,Viacheslav A. Li*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于矩阵距离度量的新方向性算法，用于改进反应堆反中微子在逆β衰变探测器中的方向重建，尤其适用于低统计量下的分段探测器，并进行了误差分析与蒙特卡洛验证。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 标准反中微子方向性重建方法在整体式与分段式探测器中存在方法论上的模糊性，亟需更鲁棒、可推广且计算高效的替代方案。

Method: 提出一种基于矩阵间'距离'度量的新方向性算法；将探测器分段数据表示为矩阵；通过比较蒙特卡洛生成的特定束流取向‘经验’数据集与模拟数据集进行验证；系统扫描并确定低计数区间的最优分段尺度。

Abstract: We present a potential improvement over the standard method developed to determine antineutrino directionality in inverse-beta-decay detectors. The previously developed method for quantifying directionality in monolithic and segmented detectors may be ambiguous in methodology. In this paper, we present a new directionality algorithm and include error analysis. We have developed a new algorithm based on a measure of ``distance'' between two matrices. We report findings for our research in reactor-antineutrino directionality, and emphasize that the algorithm has broad applications whenever one desires computationally efficient 2D pattern-matching. We treat data from detector segments in the form of a matrix. The validation of our algorithm boils down to comparing a Monte Carlo generated ``empirical'' data set to a simulated data set. ### [2] [Measurement of angular correlations inside jets induced by gluon polarization in proton-proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13.6 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03689)
*CMS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文利用13.6 TeV质子-质子碰撞数据（CMS，34.7 fb⁻¹），通过jet substructure分析（anti-kₜ + C/A declustering）构造对胶子自旋敏感的角关联观测量，首次在实验上验证了部分子喷注中胶子极化效应的存在；结果支持包含胶子自旋角关联的PYTHIA8/HERWIG7模型，显著排除忽略该效应的版本。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 检验QCD中胶子自旋在部分子级联（parton shower）中的动力学作用——特别是其诱导的角关联是否可观测，从而验证自旋-轨道耦合在强相互作用演化中的重要性。

Method: 采用anti-kₜ重建jet、Cambridge–Aachen declustering提取次级分裂结构；发展新型子结构标记技术识别g → qq̄中间态；构造对gluon helicity-dependent angular correlation敏感的观测量（如z与Δφ分布的联合分布或不对称性）。

The data correspond to an integrated luminosity of 34.7 fb$^{-1}$, collected in 2022 with the CMS detector at the LHC. The details of the parton shower are investigated using jets reconstructed with the anti-$k_\mathrm{T}$ algorithm and subsequently declustered with the Cambridge$-$Aachen algorithm. A novel analysis technique is developed to identify characteristic features of the jet substructure and to select intermediate gluon splittings into quark-antiquark pairs. An observable sensitive to gluon polarization in the parton shower is measured and compared with PYTHIA 8 and HERWIG 7 model predictions, with and without angular correlations induced by the gluon spin. The results are consistent with models that incorporate gluon polarization and strongly disfavor those that neglect them.

### [3] [Imaging baryon number density within the proton](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03730)
*Spencer R. Klein,Mathias C. Labonte,Zachary Sweger,Gerald A. Miller,Ramona Vogt*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文通过分析光子-质子碰撞中四种末态介子产生过程的横动量分布，利用傅里叶-贝塞尔变换提取相互作用系统的二维横向尺寸，首次测量了质子内重子数的空间分布，发现其横向半径为0.33–0.53 fm，显著小于电荷半径（≥0.67 fm），表明重子数高度局域于质子中心。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 不同实验手段测得的质子半径存在分歧（如电荷半径与质量半径差异），而重子数作为质子基本量子数，其空间分布尚未被直接探测，亟需新观测量以深化对质子内部结构的理解。

Method: 基于JLab CLAS实验数据，分析γp → pρ⁰、γp → pω、γp → nπ⁺、γp → pπ⁰四类排它反应的微分截面dσ/dp_T²；采用Hankel变换（即二维傅里叶-贝塞尔变换）将p_T谱映射至横向坐标空间，提取重子数密度分布的均方根半径。

Result: 重子数横向均方根半径为0.33–0.53 fm；该分布呈中心集中型，明显内缩于电荷半径（0.84 fm）和质量半径（≈0.67 fm）所定义的区域；不同末态通道结果一致，验证了重子数分布的普适性。

Conclusion: 重子数并非均匀弥散于整个质子体积，而是高度局域于核心区域，暗示手征动力学与夸克禁闭机制在中心尺度起主导作用；该结果为区分QCD模型（如Dyson-Schwinger方程、格点QCD、手征有效场论）提供了新的约束条件。

Abstract: The spatial extent of the proton is a key factor in nuclear physics. Different measurement techniques probe different aspects of the proton, yielding different radii. The mass and charge radii depend on the parton and quark distributions respectively, while the mechanical radius depends on the mass/energy distribution. Here, we probe the spatial distribution of a new proton characteristic, studying the distribution of baryon number within the proton.
  We investigate the baryon number distribution by studying four exclusive meson production channels arising from photon-proton collisions ($γp \rightarrow p ρ^0$, $γp \rightarrow p ω$, $γp \rightarrow n π^+$, and $γp \rightarrow p π^0$). The two-dimensional transverse sizes of the interacting systems are extracted by analyzing the transverse momentum, $p_T$, dependence of the meson production cross section, using Fourier-Bessel transformations. We find that baryon number is confined to a transverse radius of $0.33 - 0.53$~fm. In comparison, the transverse radius of the proton charge and mass distributions are considerably larger, at least 0.67~fm. The baryon number is concentrated in the center of the proton.

### [4] [End-to-end event reconstruction for precision physics at future colliders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04084)
*Dolores Garcia,Lena Herrmann,Gregor Krzmanc,Michele Selvaggi*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种端到端全局事件重建方法，结合几何代数Transformer网络与基于对象凝聚（object condensation）的聚类，显著提升FCC-ee上CLD探测器对Higgs等关键可观测量的重建精度与效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 未来对撞机实验（如FCC-ee）要求Higgs、电弱及味物理观测量达到前所未有的精度，而现有基于探测器特异性聚类的粒子流算法限制了探测器设计阶段的灵活性与性能上限。

Method: 采用几何代数Transformer网络建模粒子轨迹与探测器响应的几何关系，结合object condensation实现无监督式粒子级聚类，再通过专用网络进行粒子鉴别（PID）与能量回归（energy regression）。

Result: 在FCC-ee CLD全模拟数据上，相较当前最优规则型粒子流算法：相对重建效率提升10–20%，带电强子假粒子率降低达两个数量级，可见能量与不变质量分辨率提升22%。

Conclusion: 该端到端框架解耦了重建性能与探测器具体实现，为未来对撞机探测器设计提供了可快速迭代、高精度、通用性强的重建范式。

Abstract: Future collider experiments require unprecedented precision in measurements of Higgs, electroweak, and flavour observables, placing stringent demands on event reconstruction. The achievable precision on Higgs couplings scales directly with the resolution on visible final state particles and their invariant masses. Current particle flow algorithms rely on detector specific clustering, limiting flexibility during detector design. Here we present an end-to-end global event reconstruction approach that maps charged particle tracks and calorimeter and muon hits directly to particle level objects. The method combines geometric algebra transformer networks with object condensation based clustering, followed by dedicated networks for particle identification and energy regression. Our approach is benchmarked on fully simulated electron positron collisions at FCC-ee using the CLD detector concept. It outperforms the state-of-the-art rule-based algorithm by 10--20\% in relative reconstruction efficiency, achieves up to two orders of magnitude reduction in fake-particle rates for charged hadrons, and improves visible energy and invariant mass resolution by 22\%. By decoupling reconstruction performance from detector-specific tuning, this framework enables rapid iteration during the detector design phase of future collider experiments.
