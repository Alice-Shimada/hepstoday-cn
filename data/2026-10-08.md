### [2] [Sampling SU(N) gauge theory on a 2D lattice from independent plaquettes via holonomies and corner reweighting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09147)
*Javad Komijani*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种针对二维SU(N)格点规范理论（Wilson作用量）的新型生成采样方法，通过引入holonomy变量与群交换子条件，将链路变量的约束采样转化为条件采样问题，并结合解析结果与归一化流实现高效高接受率的配置生成。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在Wilson格点规范理论中，直接对link变量进行基于归一化流的生成采样困难，因plaquette分布易建模但link重构受闭合环路一致性约束；需绕过该约束以实现高效可微分采样。

Method: 将Wilson作用量重写为holonomy变量形式，利用边界条件将四角一致性约束转化为条件采样问题：即给定Z = XYX†Y† ∈ SU(N)，采样X,Y ∈ SU(N)；对SU(2)解析求解Z的密度并构造闭式采样器，对SU(3)训练归一化流求解该条件分布；独立采样plaquettes后加权修正。

Result: 获得SU(2)与SU(3)下Z = XYX†Y†的密度闭式解；构建SU(2)闭式交换子采样器与SU(3)训练型归一化流；在2×2与32×32格点、两个耦合常数下验证，单plaquette接受率>99%，有效样本量ESS > 0.5。

Conclusion: 该统计重构策略成功解耦了plaquette采样与link约束，为非阿贝尔规范场的无马尔可夫生成建模提供了可行路径，尤其适用于高维或大N拓展。

Abstract: In the Wilson formulation of lattice gauge theory, the fundamental degrees of freedom are group-valued link variables, while the action is a sum over the trace of the plaquettes, the smallest Wilson loops. For generative sampling methods such as normalizing flows, this poses a challenge: the distribution of an individual plaquette is easy to model, but mapping sampled plaquettes to the links is the obstruction. We explore a statistical way around this in two dimensions for the $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ gauge theory with the Wilson plaquette action. The action can be written in terms of holonomy variables, which determine the links once a consistency condition at the four corners of an extended lattice is satisfied. Using a boundary condition that only affects the holonomies, we trade this consistency condition for a conditional sampling problem, which reduces to sampling $X,Y\in\mathrm{SU}(N)$ given the group commutator $Z=XYX^\dagger Y^\dagger$, where $Z\in\mathrm{SU}(N)$ depends on the four corners. Plaquettes are sampled independently, and the resulting configurations carry weights due to the additional conditional sampling. We obtain the density of $Z$, which determines these weights, in closed form for $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ and $\mathrm{SU}(3)$. A normalizing flow models the single-plaquette distribution for $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ and $\mathrm{SU}(3)$; for the group-commutator problem we use a closed-form sampler for $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ and a trained normalizing flow for $\mathrm{SU}(3)$. In our tests on $2\times2$ and $32\times32$ lattices at two couplings each, per-plaquette acceptance rates exceed $99\%$ and the effective sample size of the final weights is moderate, typically above one half.

### [5] [Finite-size scaling analysis of three dimensional Z(2) and O(2) spin models with non-vanishing symmetry breaking parameter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10350)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 基于高统计精度的蒙特卡洛模拟，结合有限尺寸标度理论，拟合并识别外场H与系统尺度L依赖的主导修正项形式，验证\widetilde{z}_L^2标度律，并比较Z(2)与O(2)模型的修正适用区间。
