Method: 采用非相对论夸克-反夸克势模型：中心势为Cornell势（库仑+线性禁闭），并加入高斯抹平的自旋-自旋相互作用；数值求解薛定谔方程获得波函数；利用该波函数统一计算谱、衰变常数、轻子衰变分支比、E1/M1电磁跃迁宽度及HQET框架下的强子强衰变宽度；从实验总宽度反推HQET弱耦合常数g_H, g_S, ..., g_R；结合(J,M²)与(n_r,M²)平面的线性Regge轨迹进行态识别。

Result: 7参数模型在<1%精度下复现全部已知D/D_s激发态质量；预言f_D=231.8 MeV、f_{D_s}=252.2 MeV（与世界平均偏差0.4%）；轻子衰变、电磁跃迁与强衰变宽度均与实验一致；HQET耦合常数在D与D_s扇区高度一致（如g_T=0.42 vs. 0.39），验证味对称性；但纯c\bar{s}态无法解释D_{s0}^*(2317)极小宽度，强烈暗示其非寻常结构；D_J(3000)^0等被指认为3^1S_0、3^3S_1、3^3P_2态。

Conclusion: D/D_s介子谱与衰变可用简单非相对论势模型高度统一描述，支持HQET在粲能区的有效性与重夸克味对称性；D_{s0}^*(2317)的异常性质构成对标准夸克模型的显著挑战，指向 exotic hadronism；Regge分析为尚未确认的新态提供了明确量子数预言，可供当前实验检验。

Abstract: We decode the excited open charm mesons $D$ ($c\bar{q}$, $q=u,d$) and charm strange $D_s$ ($c\bar{s}$) families within a nonrelativistic quark-antiquark potential model based on the Cornell (Coulomb plus linear) potential supplemented by a Gaussian smeared spin-spin interaction. With only seven parameters determined by fitting the ground state masses, the numerically solved Schrödinger equation reproduces the masses of essentially every confirmed $D$ and $D_s$ state to better than $1\%$. The same wave functions are then employed to get a complete decay analysis including pseudoscalar and vector decay constants ($f_D = 231.8$~MeV, $f_{D_s} = 252.2$~MeV, the latter within $0.4\%$ of the world average), leptonic branching fractions reproducing the measured $μν$ and $τν$ modes, electromagnetic $E1$/$M1$ widths, and all kinematically open strong channels in heavy quark effective theory (HQET). Going beyond width ratios, we extract the full set of HQET couplings $g_H$, $g_S$, $g_T$, $g_X$, $g_Y$, $g_Z$, and $g_R$ directly from the measured total widths and convert our predictions into absolute widths. The $D$ and $D_s$ sector extractions of each coupling agree ($g_T = 0.42$ vs. $0.39$, $g_X = 0.22$ vs. $0.21$, $g_Y = 0.39$ vs. $0.40$), a nontrivial test of heavy quark flavor symmetry, while the pure $c\bar{s}$ picture demands a $1^3P_0$ width nearly two orders of magnitude above the observed $D_{s0}^*(2317)$ bound demanding it to be of exotic nature. Linear and parallel Regge trajectories in the $(J,M^2)$ and $(n_r,M^2)$ planes, with slopes $α\simeq 0.55-0.65$~GeV$^{-2}$ and $β\simeq 0.36 - 0.39$~GeV$^{-2}$, identify the assignments of $D_J(3000)^0$, $D_J^*(3000)^0$, and $D_2^*(3000)^0$ as the $3^1S_0$, $3^3S_1$, and $3^3P_2$ states. Our predictions provide concrete, testable targets for LHCb, BESIII, and Belle~II.
