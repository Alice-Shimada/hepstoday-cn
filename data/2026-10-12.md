### [1] [\textbf{\textsc{HepUKE}}: Uncertainty-aware Knowledge Evolution for High Energy Physics Analysis Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12862)
*Zijie Shang,Ke Li,Junkun Jiao,Shengchun Wang,Changzheng Yuan,Xiongfei Wang,Beijiang Liu,Weimin Song,Xiaoshen Kang*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文提出HepUKE——一种面向高能物理（HEP）的不确定性感知知识演化系统，利用信息熵统一建模LLM生成的统计不确定性和HEP方法论导致的系统不确定性，构建自演化的DSL知识库，并在BESIII PhysicsQA和HotpotQA上验证其检索效率与答案质量的平衡提升。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有HEP知识库将所有检索证据视为同等可信，且无法从辅助分析中持续积累知识；而HEP领域对不确定性有严格处理范式，亟需将该范式融入知识增强型智能体。

Method: 基于HEP标准不确定性处理框架，以信息熵为统一度量，量化每条证据的置信度；设计自演化的领域特定语言（DSL）知识库，结合前向边际信息增益估计与后向答案后验平稳性检验的早期停止控制器，实现高效、逻辑严谨的知识检索与写回。

Result: 在1000对BESIII PhysicsQA基准上，HepUKE减少43.0%检索轮次且保持大部分答案质量；在跨域HotpotQA上减少14.5%检索轮次，维持相近的质量–效率权衡。

Conclusion: 不确定性感知的知识演化机制可显著提升HEP智能体在少样本、高逻辑约束场景下的推理效率与鲁棒性，为科学AI提供可解释、可演化的知识管理范式。

Abstract: Knowledge bases are essential to agents for high energy physics (HEP), supplying few-shot evidence for previously unseen tasks. Their principal utility lies in strategic guidance for novel analyses rather than ready-made answers or reusable code. Prevailing systems, however, treat all retrieved evidence as equally trustworthy and fail to accrue knowledge from the analyses under assistance. In this paper, inspired by the standard HEP treatment of uncertainty, we propose \textbf{\textsc{\underline{HepUKE}}}: an \textbf{\underline{U}}ncertainty-aware \textbf{\underline{K}}nowledge \textbf{\underline{E}}volution system for \textbf{\underline{HEP}} that repurposes information entropy to unify statistical uncertainty from LLM generation and systematic uncertainty from HEP methodological choices. Upon it, we construct a self-evolving domain-specific-language (DSL) knowledge base and release a large-scale expert-annotated HEP physics-analysis benchmark. Per-evidence confidence is quantified via the information entropy of sampled DSL generations: high-confidence precedents constrain program synthesis, whereas analyses lacking close precedents are validated and written back, sustaining continual knowledge evolution. To handle the highly redundant yet logically stringent nature of HEP knowledge, we introduce an early-stopping controller that fuses a backward stationarity test on the answer posterior with a forward marginal information gain estimate. On the 1,000-pair BESIII PhysicsQA benchmark, HepUKE reduces retrieval rounds by 43.0\% while preserving most answer quality, and on the out-of-domain HotpotQA benchmark, it reduces retrieval rounds by 14.5\% with a similar quality--efficiency trade-off. The code and datasets used in this work are available at https://anonymous.4open.science/r/HepUKE-32FF.
