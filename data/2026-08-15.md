*Juan P. Garcés,Jisuke Kubo,Manfred Lindner,Markus Reinig*

Main category: hep-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出一个基于经典尺度不变二次引力耦合强相互作用隐藏扇区的统一框架，用以同时解释电弱能标起源、宇宙暴胀和暗物质。R²项实现Starobinsky暴胀，隐藏扇区禁闭动力学生成普朗克能标并诱导希格斯质量，从而触发电弱对称性破缺；R²对应的标量场通过能量-动量张量普适耦合完成再加热，并引力冻结产生暗物质。研究了三种典型隐藏扇区实现：η'介子、矢量玻色子及带电π介子作为暗物质候选者，并计算其遗迹丰度与暗辐射。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 统一解释电弱能标、暴胀与暗物质三大未解问题，避免标准模型与ΛCDM模型中的人为能标输入，追求基于尺度不变性的自洽紫外完备理论。

Method: 构建经典尺度不变的二次引力理论（含R²项）耦合强耦合隐藏规范扇区；利用隐藏扇区的渐近自由与禁闭动力学生成有效普朗克质量；通过Weyl共形变换与曲率-物质耦合导出引力诱导的希格斯质量项；采用能量-动量张量耦合的标量再加热机制，并运用引力冻结（gravitational freeze-in）计算暗物质产额。

The $R^2$ term naturally realizes Starobinsky inflation, while confinement in the hidden sector dynamically generates the Planck scale and triggers electroweak symmetry breaking through a gravitationally induced Higgs mass generation mechanism. The scalar degree of freedom associated with the $R^2$ term subsequently reheats both the visible and hidden sectors through universal couplings to the energy-momentum tensor, leading to the gravitational freeze-in production of hidden-sector states. We investigate three representative realizations of the hidden sector in which the dark matter candidate is either a hidden $η'$ meson, a hidden vector boson, or charged hidden pions, and derive the corresponding dark matter and dark radiation relic abundances.

### [2] [Probing CP-violating top-quark dipole moments with tomographic observables](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12463)
*Maxim Perelstein,Minal Shaik,Taewook Youn*

Main category: hep-ph

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探测顶夸克耦合中的CP破坏是检验超出标准模型新物理的关键途径；传统观测量对电偶极矩和色电偶极矩算符的灵敏度受限，亟需更高效的信息提取方法。

Method: 基于双轻子末态的角分布构建量子层析观测量，利用洛伦兹与自旋对称性筛选CP奇异性观测量；结合自旋密度矩阵重建与运动学信息，构造统计最优的复合观测量；采用快速探测器模拟进行灵敏度外推。

Abstract: Quantum tomography program reconstructs the full spin density matrix of top-quark pairs from dilepton angular correlations at both electron-positron and hadron colliders. We use symmetry arguments to identify tomographic observables sensitive to CP violation in top quark couplings. As a concrete example, we study the sensitivity of these observables to (chromo-)electric dipole moment operators at the LHC and the FCC-ee. We also construct the optimal observable, which combines tomographic and kinematic information to achieve statistically optimal sensitivity for each operator and production process. The analysis is based on fast detector simulation of top pair production and decay in the dilepton channel. We project a $95\%$ CL sensitivity to new-physics scales (the inverse dipole couplings) of $10-15$ TeV for the electroweak electric dipoles at the FCC-ee ($365$ GeV, $3~{\rm ab}^{-1}$) and of 40 (200) TeV for the top chromo-electric dipole moment at the LHC Run 2 (HL-LHC). This sensitivities exceed those of the traditional observables currently used by experiments to use for these operators, indicating the power of the tomographic approach to search for CP violation.

### [3] [Soft Collinear Effective Theory for Heavy QCD Axions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12467)
*Deepanshu Bisht,Sabyasachi Chakraborty,Siddhartha Karmakar,Atanu Samanta*

Main category: hep-ph

TL;DR: 本文构建了重QCD轴子的软-共线有效理论（SCET）框架，针对两种低能实现情形，以B→Ka衰变为基准过程，系统分析了软贡献与旁观者散射贡献的动力学起源、因子化结构及相对大小，并据此给出轴子衰变常数f_a的实验约束。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 重QCD轴子在罕见B介子衰变中可能产生可观测信号，但其有效理论描述需严格处理多尺度问题；现有工作未系统区分不同紫外起源导致的低能算符结构差异，尤其缺乏对旁观者散射在轴子产生中作用的定量评估。

Method: 采用两步匹配策略：首先在弱有效理论（WET）中消除冗余算符并识别出新的维度七轴子-胶子导数算符O_{∂ag}；继而在μ～m_b尺度将其匹配至SCET_I，推导软重叠项与旁观者散射项的因子化表达式；对第二种实现，通过RGE演化至高标度并重新匹配，引入直接b→sa电流算符，对比两种情形下软与旁观者贡献的幂次地位与数值权重。

Result: 在第一种实现中，旁观者散射贡献达软重叠项的～25%，且与之同为leading-power；在第二种实现中，软形式因子主导，旁观者项压低至～6–7%；由此确定旁观者散射显著的条件为：轴子主要通过aG\widetilde{G}耦合在m_b附近生成，而非由高标度b→sa算符主导。

Conclusion: 旁观者散射在重轴子B→Ka衰变中不可忽略，其重要性高度依赖于轴子耦合的紫外起源；所建立的SCET框架为区分不同轴子模型提供了关键动力学判据，并给出了f_a的严格上下界约束。

Abstract: We develop a soft-collinear effective theory (SCET) framework for heavy QCD axion, considering two low-energy realizations and taking $B\to Ka$ as a benchmark mode. In the first realization, $aG\widetilde G$ is assumed to be the only independent axion interaction at the scale $μ\sim m_b$. The spectator contribution arises at the same order in the power expansion as the soft-overlap term and amounts to approximately $25\%$ of the soft contribution. In the second realization, the Wilson coefficient of $aG\widetilde G$ is assumed to be present above the electroweak scale. Renormalization-group evolution and matching then induce a direct $b\to sa$ operator, which subsequently results in a dominant soft form-factor contribution, whereas the gluonic spectator term turns out to be numerically subleading ($\sim 6-7\%$). We thus identify the conditions under which spectator scattering becomes relevant for heavy-axion production in rare $B$-meson decays. Finally, we derive the corresponding bounds on the axion decay constant $f_a$ for both realizations and compare their phenomenological implications.

### [4] [Effects of Quantum Spin-Connection Foam in the Solar System, Galaxies, and the Universe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12404)
*Igor V. Kanatchikov,Valery A. Kholodnyi*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: 本文提出量子自旋联络泡沫（源自广义相对论的预正则量子化）可解释宇宙学常数的小值与星系旋转曲线平坦性，导出含基本加速度标度 a_* 的修正牛顿势，并通过与强相互作用质量隙关联，使 a_* 与MOND经验标度一致；同时 a_*² 正比于宇宙学常数，给出合理数值；在非相对论近似下，由预正则量子引力最简解的平均场加速度导出类MOND动力学（具理论特异的插值函数）；预言该效应或可在太阳系及实验室探测。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解释观测到的小宇宙学常数和星系尺度上牛顿动力学的异常（如平坦旋转曲线），寻求量子引力效应在低能宏观尺度的可观测表现，并建立其与标准模型强相互作用能标的联系。

Method: 基于广义相对论的预正则量子化框架，引入量子自旋联络泡沫作为量子引力介质；在非相对论近似下，计算其诱导的平均场加速度；构造含基本加速度标度 a_* = 8πGℏκ 的修正牛顿势；将参数 κ 与纯杨-米尔斯理论的质量隙（hadronic scale）关联；推导修正动力学并对比MOND经验公式。

Result: 得到一个含基本加速度标度 a_* 的修正牛顿势；a_* 由 κ 关联至强子质量标度，数值与MOND的 Milgrom 加速度 a₀ ≈ 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s² 相容；a_*² 正比于宇宙学常数 Λ，给出 Λ ∼ 10⁻⁵² m⁻² 量级的合理值；在特定平均场近似下导出类MOND动力学，但插值函数具有理论起源而非唯象设定；预言太阳系内及实验室中可能的可观测效应。

Conclusion: 量子自旋联络泡沫作为预正则量子引力的低能有效表征，可统一解释宇宙学常数问题与星系尺度动力学异常，其特征加速度标度 a_* 源于量子引力与强相互作用的深层联系，为量子引力提供潜在的低能可观测窗口。

Abstract: We argue that effects of the quantum spin-connection foam, which describes quantum gravity according to the precanonical quantization of General Relativity, may already be observed in the form of the small cosmological constant and a modification of Newtonian dynamics at small accelerations, manifested in the flat rotation curves of galaxies. We obtain a modification of the Newtonian potential that takes into account the existence of a fundamental small acceleration scale, $a_* = 8πG\hbar\varkappa$, where $\varkappa$ is a parameter with the dimensions of inverse spatial volume that appears on dimensional grounds. The connection between $\varkappa$ and the hadronic scale of the mass gap in the pure Yang-Mills sector of the Standard Model leads to an estimated value of $a_*$ compatible with the Milgromian acceleration scale in MOND. The connection between $a_*^2$ and the cosmological constant leads to a realistic value of the latter. Milgromian MOND, together with a theoretically distinct interpolating function, is derived under the assumption that classical dynamics is modified by the mean-field acceleration calculated from the simplest solution of precanonical quantum gravity in the nonrelativistic approximation. We also indicate that the effects of Newtonian dynamics modified by the spin-connection foam may be observable in the Solar System and even in laboratory experiments.
