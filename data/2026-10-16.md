Motivation: 解决偶极子减除算法中减除截面存在真实截面所没有的虚假奇异性问题，该问题在偶极子蒙特卡洛演化和匹配算法中导致统计收敛变差或依赖人为调节参数（如生成截断）

Method: 在减除截面中引入额外的收敛因子，显式地压制来自Born矩阵元的虚假奇异性；解析完成相空间积分，导出修正后的偶极子插入算符

Result: 修正后的偶极子插入算符在数值上被验证可消除原方案中对生成截断的对数依赖，显著提升统计收敛性

Conclusion: 收敛因子是系统性处理偶极子减除中虚假奇异性的一般方法，为高精度QCD蒙特卡洛模拟（尤其是减除- shower 匹配）提供了更鲁棒的理论基础

Abstract: In the dipole subtraction algorithm, the real-virtual cancellation in NLO calculations of arbitrary observables is implemented by defining a subtraction cross section that matches all of the singularities of the real emission cross section. The converse, however, is not true: the subtraction cross section contains additional singularities, coming from the underlying Born matrix element, that the real cross section does not. We call these spurious singularities. If used as originally proposed, this is not a problem, because the value of the observable damps these singularities, but in applications such as dipole showers and matching algorithms, it can lead to poor statistical convergence, or dependence on unphysical regulators such as technical or generation cuts. In this paper, we show that additional factors, which we call convergence factors, can be applied to the subtraction cross section to damp these spurious singularities and improve the convergence. We perform the required phase-space integrals to yield modified dipole insertion operators, and verify numerically that they indeed remove the logarithmic dependence on generation cuts that arises in their absence. This paper is dedicated to the memory of Stefano Catani, with whom the work was originally started.
