### [1] [Hadronic Mono-Z Dark Matter Sensitivity with Flow Matching on CMS Open Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02923)
*Hitesh Rasineni,Bhavishya Chebrolu*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文基于CMS Run 2015D HTMHT开放数据（2.26 fb⁻¹），开展强子型单Z（hadronic mono-Z）暗物质产生过程的投影灵敏度研究；采用条件流匹配连续归一化流（CFM-CNF）建模背景，结合哨兵插补、固定验证集与最小背景截断等策略抑制偏差；基准分析对三个简化模型给出2.89σ–7.62σ预期显著性，消融实验证明额外喷注运动学贡献主导区分能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 提升强子型mono-Z末态中暗物质信号对标准模型背景（尤其是QCD多喷注及Z+jets）的区分能力，克服传统方法在高维相空间建模不足与特征缺失导致的偏差问题。

Method: 使用条件流匹配连续归一化流（CFM-CNF）在HTMHT数据上无监督学习背景分布；引入哨兵值（sentinel imputation）处理未定义角变量（如Δφ(jet,Z)）；固定训练/验证索引以避免数据泄露；设定背景产量下限20事件以稳定工作点选择；对模拟信号施加离线触发代理（offline trigger proxy）校准效率；通过消融实验量化额外喷注动力学（extra-jet kinematics）的信息增益。

Result: 基准分析对三个简化模型分别获得2.89σ、7.62σ和7.41σ的预期统计显著性；消融实验显示移除额外喷注运动学信息后显著性下降53–71%，证实其关键判别作用；所有结果均为投影灵敏度，未进行数据盲分析。

Conclusion: CFM-CNF结合鲁棒特征工程可在有限开放数据下实现高精度背景建模，额外喷注拓扑是hadronic mono-Z通道的核心鉴别维度；该方法为未来LHC开放数据分析与轻质量暗物质搜寻提供了可复现、偏差可控的新范式。

Abstract: We present a projected sensitivity study for hadronic mono-$Z$ dark-matter production using CMS Run~2015D HTMHT open data corresponding to 2.256382381~\invfb, from which 1{,}439{,}523 events satisfy the hadronic mono-$Z$ selection. Backgrounds are modelled with a conditional flow-matching continuous normalizing flow trained on the selected HTMHT events and evaluated on a held-out validation split reweighted to the full selected population. To mitigate artifacts from missing-object features and avoid in-sample scoring bias we apply sentinel imputation for undefined angular features, persist the train/validation split indices, and enforce a minimum reported background yield of 20 events when selecting the working point. A signal-side offline trigger proxy is applied to the simulated signal before scoring. Under this procedure the baseline analysis yields expected significances of 2.89$σ$, 7.62$σ$, and 7.41$σ$ for three simplified-model benchmarks. An ablation study that removes the detailed extra-jet kinematics reduces the expected significance by 53--71\%, indicating that extra-jet topology carries substantial discriminating power in the hadronic mono-$Z$ channel. These results are projected sensitivities (no unblinding performed); the limitations and reproducibility of the study are discussed in Sections limitations and reproducibility.

Result: 该模型在m_χ ≈ 400 GeV、m_a ≈ 1 GeV处可实现热冻结丰度；其核反冲谱形状导致LZ数据中局部显著性达2.6–3.2σ；接触项L₄给出当前LZ分析中最大的局域显著性之一。

Conclusion: LZ单事例可由弹性赝标量耦合暗物质自然解释，无需依赖不确定的高速暗物质分布；该模型具有可检验的紫外结构（矢量型重夸克、轻赝标中介子），并与热历史自洽。

Abstract: LUX-ZEPLIN (LZ) has reported one event compatible with a $248\,\mathrm{keV}$ nuclear recoil. While initial attention has focused on inelastic dark matter, that interpretation depends sensitively on the uncertain high-speed tail of the Galactic halo. We develop instead an elastic realization of the pseudoscalar-pseudoscalar interaction $L_4=(\barχiγ^5χ)(\bar N iγ^5N)$, whose contact templates give one of the largest local significances identified by LZ. The minimal model presented involves primarily Dirac fermion dark matter, and a complex scalar leading to a pseudoscalar mediator. The minimal gauge-invariant UV completion also requires a single multi-TeV vector-like heavy quark. A representative model which realizes thermal freeze-out has $m_χ=400~{\rm GeV}$ and $m_a=1~{\rm GeV}$. The simplest model breaks from the isoscalar/isovector assumption, and the response-level recoil spectrum lies between LZ's elastic $O_4^v$ and contact $L_4^v$ templates, suggesting a local significance of $2.6$-$3.2σ$.
