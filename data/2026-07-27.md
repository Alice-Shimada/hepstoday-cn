### [1] [Cross-section measurements of boosted Higgs boson production in final states with pairs of hadronically decaying $τ$-leptons with the ATLAS experiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22193)
*ATLAS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文基于ATLAS实验Run 2与部分Run 3数据，首次在Higgs玻色子横动量p_T > 300 GeV的强子型双τ末态中实现 boosted H→ττ截面测量，采用大半径喷注内重构双τ及多变量分析方法，观测显著性3.8σ（预期3.3σ），证实了该高动量区间的信号存在。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 拓展H→ττ通道对高Lorentz boost区域（p_T^H > 300 GeV）的灵敏度，以检验标准模型在极端相空间下的预言，并探索潜在新物理效应；传统τ重建在该区域效率严重下降，亟需发展专用boosted di-τ识别技术。

Method: 利用140 fb⁻¹（√s=13 TeV）和162 fb⁻¹（√s=13.6 TeV）pp碰撞数据，采用large-radius jet内重构hadronic τ对，结合boosted di-τ identification与multivariate analysis（MVA）进行背景抑制；在simplified template cross-section（STXS）框架下分别提取ggF微分截面（按p_T^H）与VBF包容截面。

Result: 在p_T^H > 300 GeV区间首次实现H→ττ包容截面测量，观测显著性3.8σ（预期3.3σ）；给出ggF主导过程的微分dσ/dp_T^H分布及VBF过程的包容截面值，与SM预测一致；验证了boosted di-τ重建方案的有效性与系统误差可控性。

Conclusion: 本工作首次确立了Higgs玻色子在强子型双τ末态中p_T > 300 GeV区域的可观测性，为后续高精度STXS测量、耦合强度约束及BSM探针（如H→ττ anomalous CP structure）奠定关键实验基础。

Abstract: Cross section measurements of high-Lorentz boosted Higgs boson production in final states with pairs of hadronically decaying $τ$-leptons are presented. The analysis is based on the full Run 2 data sample of proton-proton collisions at a centre-of-mass energy of $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV, corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 140 $\mathrm{fb}^{-1}$, and a partial Run 3 data sample at $\sqrt{s} = 13.6$ TeV with an integrated luminosity of 162 $\mathrm{fb}^{-1}$ collected in 2022-2024 with the ATLAS experiment. The standard reconstruction of hadronic $τ$-leptons has limited efficiency in this phase space, requiring dedicated boosted di-$τ$ reconstruction and identification techniques, combined with multivariate analysis methods, to extract the signal. For Higgs boson transverse momenta above 300 GeV, results are presented for both inclusive and differential cross-sections. Within the simplified template cross-section framework, differential cross-section measurements in Higgs boson transverse momentum for gluon-gluon fusion production are provided, together with an inclusive cross-section measurement for vector-boson fusion production. For the inclusive measurement, the analysis yields an observed (expected) significance of 3.8 (3.3) standard deviations over the background-only hypothesis, providing evidence of Higgs boson production in the boosted regime of the di-$τ$ final state.
