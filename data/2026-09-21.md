### [2] [Dark photons in exotic Higgs boson decays at FCC-ee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21648)
*Axel Gallén,Sarah Ben Abdesselem,Giulia Ripellino,Rebeca Gonzalez Suarez*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文在FCC-ee的电子-正电子对撞阶段，针对暗光子衰变为准直、位移的μ⁺μ⁻对这一新物理信号开展前瞻性探测研究；利用Higgs工厂运行（√s = 240 GeV）全积分亮度，结合Z→jj与H→γ_D γ_D → 4μ双轻双重事例拓扑，通过运动学选发实现零本底高效率筛选，并给出Higgs到暗光子分支比随m_{γ_D}与ε的预期排除限。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探测轻质量、弱耦合暗光子（γ_D）是解决暗物质与轻子反常等谜题的关键途径之一；FCC-ee作为超高精度Higgs工厂，具备极优的不变质量分辨率与低本底环境，适于探测Higgs稀有不可见或半可见衰变中产生的长寿命暗光子。

Method: 基于蒙特卡洛模拟构建完整探测器响应模型；设计包含位移顶点重建、角度准直性（ΔR(μμ) < 0.1）、飞行距离cτ ∈ [1, 1000] mm、以及Z-jet与μ-pair动量平衡约束的多级运动学选择；采用形状拟合与CLs方法设定95%置信度上限。

Conclusion: 该分析证实FCC-ee在Higgs工厂阶段对长寿命暗光子具有独特探测量能；所提位移μμ+双喷注拓扑为Higgs不可见衰变中隐含新轻介子提供了高灵敏度、低系统误差的新探针。

Abstract: A prospective search for dark photons decaying into collimated, displaced muon pairs is presented for the electron-positron stage of the Future Circular Collider, FCC-ee. Assuming the full luminosity of the Higgs run at 240 GeV, the study probes a broad range of dark photon masses and couplings. The signature comprises two jets from a Z boson decay and two muon pairs from dark photons produced in Higgs decays. A kinematics-based selection achieves zero background while retaining high signal efficiency. Expected limits are set on the Higgs boson branching ratio to dark photons as a function of the dark photon mass and coupling.

### [3] [Search for resonant and nonresonant production of pairs of dijet resonances with b jets in the final state in proton-proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21850)
*CMS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文在13 TeV质子-质子对撞中利用CMS探测器136 fb⁻¹数据，首次开展针对成对产生、全分辨、含b夸克的双喷注共振态（即四喷注末态）的LHC搜寻；区分非共振与共振产生机制，未发现显著信号，给出四喷注/双喷注共振态截面上限，并排除0.5–0.8 TeV顶夸克超对称伙伴及2–7 TeV重二夸克。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索超出标准模型的新物理，特别是成对产生的含b夸克双喷注共振态（如超对称顶夸克对、二夸克态等），此前尚无LHC实验对此类全分辨四喷注末态进行过专门搜寻。

Method: 基于CMS探测器数据，利用喷注能量校准、b标记（b-tagging）、不变质量重建与组合优化技术，分别构建非共振（连续四喷注背景建模）与共振（四喷注整体质量+两对喷注子质量联合拟合）分析通道；采用渐近似然比检验设定95%置信度上限。

Result: 非共振道最大偏离为平均双喷注质量1.0 TeV处，局部显著性2.1σ；共振道最大偏离为四喷注共振质量3.1 TeV且双喷注子质量1.3 TeV处，局部显著性2.6σ；排除RPV顶 squark 0.5–0.8 TeV；二夸克模型中2–7 TeV质量区间被95% CL排除。

Conclusion: 未观测到统计学显著的新物理信号；该分析建立了首例针对全分辨含b双共振四喷注末态的严格限制，显著拓展了超对称与强耦合新物理（如二夸克）的参数空间约束。

Abstract: A search is presented for pair-produced dijet resonances of identical mass, each decaying into a bottom (b) quark and a light-flavor quark. The search uses a data sample corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 136 fb$^{-1}$ collected with the CMS detector in proton-proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV. Results are presented separately for nonresonant production and for the case where the four-jet production proceeds via an intermediate resonant state. Upper limits are reported on the production of four-jet and dijet resonances with b jets in the final state. This is the first LHC search for resonant production of pairs of fully resolved dijet resonances with b jets in the final state, in which all resonances considered correspond to non-standard-model states. For nonresonant production, the most significant excess occurs at an average dijet mass of 1.0 TeV, with a local significance of 2.1 standard deviations. Additionally, the results exclude pair production of top squarks with masses between 0.5 and 0.8 TeV, extending previous searches for $R$-parity-violating decays to strange and b quarks. In the search for resonant production, the most significant excess occurs at a four-jet resonance mass of 3.1 TeV and a dijet resonance mass of 1.3 TeV, with a local significance of 2.6 standard deviations. The results are also used to set limits at 95% confidence level on the diquark-to-diquarks model and exclude the heavier diquark masses between 2 and 7 TeV.

</details>
